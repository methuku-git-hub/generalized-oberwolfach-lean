12 Release criteria and scope

A full certificate must contain a theorem whose type is definitionally identical to Oberwolfach.MainTheorem, with no additional theorem hypotheses and no unapproved axioms. The permitted foundational axioms are propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. Their presence is disclosed; “no additional axioms” does not mean an axiom-free foundation.

The verification procedure builds the modules, enumerates the exported project theorems and their transitive axioms, and replays declarations with Lean’s kernel. Kernel replay is not an independent implementation of the kernel. Faithfulness of the definitions to the manuscript remains a separate mathematical review. In particular, no successful build or green blueprint node certifies an unimplemented sampling, absorption or literature theorem.

The present project has no unconditional Lean proof of the main theorem. The ready-to-share package must therefore be described as a blueprint and partial formal verification, not as a certificate of the paper.
