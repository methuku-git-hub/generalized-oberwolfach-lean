Lemma 11.4 Protected regular edge partition
✓
#

Uses

  • Theorem 6.4
  • Lemma 7.7
  • Lemma 7.9
  • Lemma 7.10

Lean declarations

  • Oberwolfach.protectedRegularPartition_highProbability
  • Oberwolfach.protectedRegularPartition_family_highProbability
  • Oberwolfach.regularDegree_colourDensities

Fix \(\alpha {\gt}0\), \(k\ge 1\), positive \(r_a,\xi _a\), and an integer \(B\ge 0\). There is \(N\) depending only on these data such that the following holds for every \(n\ge N\). Let \(G\) be an even \(D\)-regular graph of order \(n\) with \(D\ge (1/2+\alpha )n\). Let \(\sum _a m_a=D/2\) and \(\rho _a=2m_a/D\ge r_a\). Fix sets \(I_b\) with \(\sum _b|I_b|\le \alpha n/100000\) before colouring the edges independently with probabilities \((\rho _a)_a\), obtaining \(S_a^0\). With probability at least \(1-n^{-10}\), for every later family of subgraphs \(A_a\subseteq S_a^0\) with \(\sum _a e(A_a)\le B\), there is an exact edge partition \((S_a)_a\) of \(G\) such that

\[ d_{S_a}(v)=2m_a,\qquad A_a\subseteq S_a,\qquad S_a[I_b]=S_a^0[I_b],\qquad \Delta (S_a\triangle S_a^0)\le \xi _a n. \]

The more general checked form protects any fixed graph of maximum degree at most \(\alpha n/100000\), and every later graph of maximum degree at most \(B\).

Manuscript label: lem:family:partition.

Proof ▼

Use the common degree, cut and balancing event. Small cut sides are handled by subtracting omitted labels from the row or column degree; large sides use the quadratic cut estimate. Set \(\delta _a=\min \{ \alpha /32,\rho _a/512\} \) and \(h_a=\alpha \rho _a/128\). The raw cut is at least \(2h_an\min \{ |S|,|T|\} \). Excluding protected edges and reversing directions loses at most

\[ (\rho _a\alpha n/100000+n^{3/5}+B+4n^{4/5})\min \{ |S|,|T|\} . \]

The checked numerical choices make this at most \(h_an\min \{ |S|,|T|\} \). Choose \(q=\lfloor q_*n\rfloor \) with the source bounds on \(q_*\). All rounding and normalized-demand margins are proved uniformly before \(n,G,(m_a),(\rho _a)\) are specified. Apply the deterministic partition with real error \(e=5n^{4/5}\) and obtain the bound \(4q+2e\). The probability estimates are first proved with failure \(n^{-12}\); their union has failure at most \(n^{-10}\). Project out the auxiliary directions only after the universal quantifier over later protected graphs.

Lemma 11.6 Ordinary threshold lower bound of one half
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 1.2

Lean declarations

  • Oberwolfach.cycleThreshold_half_le
  • Oberwolfach.allowedThreshold_half_le
  • Oberwolfach.cycle_divisible_dense_counterexample
  • Oberwolfach.HasCycleDecomposition.closed_set_edge_divisible

For every \(r\ge 3\), \(\delta _r\ge 1/2\). Thus \(\delta _S\ge 1/2\) for every nonempty allowed set \(S\subseteq \{ 3,4,\ldots \} \).

Manuscript label: prop:four:lower.

Proof ▼

For \(t=2rk\), use \(K_{t-1}\) and \(K_{t+1}\) minus a Hamilton cycle on disjoint vertex sets. The resulting graph is \((t-2)\)-regular on \(2t\) vertices and is \(C_r\)-divisible. Its first component has \(\binom {t-1}{2}\) edges, not divisible by \(r\). A cycle decomposition would force divisibility of the edge count in every closed vertex set. The degree ratio tends to \(1/2\). The formal construction chooses \(k\) explicitly for each requested order and each density below \(1/2\). This checks only the half-density consequence of the cited proposition.

For odd \(r\ge 3\), \(\delta _r\ge r/(2r-2)\); in particular, \(\delta _3\ge 3/4\). Lemma 11.9 Four-cycle lower bound and local threshold comparisons
✓

We have \(\delta _4\ge 2/3\). For \(t=4k+7\), take cliques \(A,C\) of size \(t\) and an independent set \(B\) of size \(t-1\), add all \(AB\) and \(BC\) edges, and delete a six-cycle inside \(C\). ✓
#

For every even length at least four and positive degree margin, positive lower and upper weight constants are fixed first. For every positive accuracy there is a degree tolerance, then for each separation distance there is an order threshold. The resulting weights on ordered tuples are zero unless every ordinary edge and the closing edge to the permuted first vertex exist and all labels are separated.
