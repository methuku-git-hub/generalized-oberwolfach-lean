Fix integers \(k\ge 2\), \(B\ge 0\) and real numbers \(K,a,\gamma {\gt}0\). There is \(n_0\) such that the following holds whenever \(n\ge n_0\) and \(n\le D\le n^B\). Let \(\mathcal H\) be a finite indexed \(k\)-uniform hypergraph with no isolated vertices, at most \(n^B\) vertices, maximum degree at most \(D\) and maximum codegree at most \(KD/n\). For a family of at most \(n^B\) functions \(w_i:E(\mathcal H)\to [0,b_i]\), where \(b_i{\gt}0\), put \(W_i=\sum _e w_i(e)\) and suppose \(W_i\ge a b_iDn\). There is a matching \(M\) such that

\[ |w_i(M)-W_i/D|{\lt}\gamma W_i/D \qquad \text{for every prescribed }i. \]

The threshold is chosen before the hypergraph and the weight functions.

Proof ▼

Choose \(\eta =\min \{ \gamma /20,1/100\} \) and \(h=\lfloor n^{1/(64k)}\rfloor \) using the integer root operation. Take \(p=h^{16}\) vertex parts, \(q=\lfloor D/h^{16k-12}\rfloor \) edge parts, cell degree bound \(d=\lceil (1+4\eta )h^4\rceil \) and \(\ell =\lceil (1+\eta )d\rceil \) colours. The six exponent bounds for the three common events are at most \(-ch\) for a fixed \(c{\gt}0\). Their union bounds are dominated by a fixed polynomial in \(h\) times \(e^{-ch}\), and hence are less than one for a threshold chosen before the host and tests. The integer inequalities give \(D\le p^{k-1}q\ell \le (1+8\eta )D\). The accumulated weight error and this normalization error are together less than \(10\eta W_i/D\). All integer rounding, positivity and asymptotic estimates are proved in Lean.

For finite sets \(E,J\), nonnegative weights \(w:E\to \mathbb R\) with positive total mass and vectors \(z_e\in \mathbb R^J\), there are nonnegative weights \(w'\) on at most \(|J|+1\) original indices such that

\[ \sum _e w'(e)=\sum _e w(e),\qquad \sum _e w'(e)z_e=\sum _e w(e)z_e. \]

Moreover, \(w(e)=0\) implies \(w'(e)=0\).

Proof ▼

Normalize by the positive total mass and apply the proved Caratheodory convex-hull theorem to the vectors indexed by the original positive support. Choose original indices for its affine-independent representation and aggregate their coefficients. The affine dimension bounds the new support.

The following theorem completes the fractional-resource rounding argument. Preparation, cover-down, terminal absorption and the main decomposition theorem still require their graph-specific proofs.

Fix integers \(r\ge 2\), \(B\ge 1\) and \(K,a,\gamma {\gt}0\). There are \(\eta _0{\gt}0\) and \(n_0\) such that the following holds for \(n\ge n_0\). Let an indexed hypergraph have at most \(n^B\) vertices and edge sizes between \(2\) and \(r\). Suppose nonnegative edge weights \(w\) have vertex loads in \([1-\eta _0,1+\eta _0]\) and pair loads at most \(K/n\). Let \(\mathcal S\) be at most \(n^B\) vertex subsets of size at least \(an\), and let \(\mathcal F\) be at most \(n^B\) fixed functions \(g:E\to [0,1]\) with \(W_g=\sum _e g(e)w(e)\ge an\). There is a matching \(M\) such that

\[ |S\setminus V(M)|\le \gamma |S|\quad (S\in \mathcal S),\qquad (1-\gamma )W_g\le g(M)\le (1+\gamma )W_g\quad (g\in \mathcal F). \]

The thresholds are independent of the number of original edge identifiers.

Manuscript label: lem:new:weighted-resource-matching.

Proof ▼

Use \(\eta _0=\min \{ \gamma /20,1/100\} \). Preserve all vertex, ordered-pair and prescribed-weight sums on at most \(4n^{2B}\) indices. Replace each weight by \(\lfloor Tw'(e)\rfloor \) copies, where \(T=n^{2B+2}\), and pad each copy to rank \(r\) with private vertices. Original degrees lie between \((1-2\eta _0)T\) and \((1+\eta _0)T\); private degrees are one. Take \(D=\lceil (1+\eta _0)T\rceil \). The enlarged vertex set and test family have size at most \(n^{3B+4}\), and the pair loads are at most \((K+1)D/n\). The functions \(e\mapsto |e\cap S|\) and the lifts of \(g\) satisfy the uniform weighted theorem’s mass bound with coefficient \(a/(4r)\). Apply that theorem with relative error \(\eta _0\) and project its matching. Nonempty original edges make this projection injective, so selected test sums are preserved exactly. The proved floor and normalization bounds give the displayed conclusions. The empty vertex set is handled separately.
