4 Checked transfers used in iteration

Lemma 4.1 Local-scale conversion
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Oberwolfach.local_scale_error

Suppose \(\epsilon {\gt}0\), \(C,\sigma ,n\ge 0\), \(n\ge \epsilon m/2\) and \(2C\sigma /\epsilon \le \delta \). Then \(\delta n+C\sigma m\le 2\delta n\).

Proof ▼

Multiply the size inequality by \(C\sigma \) and the margin inequality by \(\epsilon n\). Both multipliers are nonnegative. These give \(C\sigma m\le \delta n\).

Lemma 4.2 Protected restriction
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Oberwolfach.protected_restriction

If \(C\cap T=C_{\mathrm{next}}\) and \(E\cap T=\varnothing \), then \((C\setminus E)\cap T=C_{\mathrm{next}}\).

Proof ▼

Check membership of an arbitrary element. This applies to sets of typed occurrences as well as sets of ordinary edges; it does not identify the two representations.

Lemma 4.3 Large-cut normalization: the scalar step
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Oberwolfach.large_cut_stability_arithmetic

Let \(a_1,a_2{\gt}0\) and \(x,y,b\ge 0\). If \(e\ge h\min (a_2x,a_1y)\) and \(e'\ge e-b\min (x,y)\), then

\[ e'\ge \left(h-\frac b{\min (a_1,a_2)}\right)\min (a_2x,a_1y). \]

Manuscript label: lem:pr:cut-stability.

Proof ▼

Use \(\min (a_1,a_2)\min (x,y)\le \min (a_2x,a_1y)\). Only this scalar implication is formalised here. The graph-theoretic premise counting the deleted edges remains to be connected to the maximum-degree hypothesis in Lean.

Lemma 4.4 One common event and later choices
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Oberwolfach.common_event_adaptive_choice

Let \(E\subseteq \Omega \) be one event. Suppose \(\forall \omega \in E\ \forall x,\ A(\omega ,x)\Rightarrow B(\omega ,x)\). If \(c:\Omega \to X\) satisfies \(A(\omega ,c(\omega ))\) for every \(\omega \in E\), then \(E\subseteq \{ \omega :B(\omega ,c(\omega ))\} \).

Proof ▼

Substitute \(x=c(\omega )\) on the same event. This is a deterministic logical implication. It does not establish the probability of \(E\), measurability or the manuscript’s sampling estimates.
