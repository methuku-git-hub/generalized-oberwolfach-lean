  28 theorem ordinaryPhysicalGraph_disjoint (Q R:SimpleGraph V) (H K:V→U→Prop):
  29     Disjoint (ordinaryPhysicalGraph Q H) (ordinaryPhysicalGraph R K)↔
  30       Disjoint Q R ∧ ∀ a u,¬(H a u∧K a u):=by

  35     · exact (disjoint_iff_inf_le.mp h) (v:=Sum.inl a) (w:=Sum.inr u) hau
