  13     1-s-t-a-b=θ+(1-θ)*rare:=by

  16   have hcm:0<c-1:=by linarith

  19   have hf:(1-θ)*(ℓ+f+rare)=1-θ:=by rw [hfractions];ring

  21   nlinarith [ht,ha,hf]
