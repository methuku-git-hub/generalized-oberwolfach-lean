   4 /-! Exact wheel decompositions can be restricted to a typed subhost when
   5 all their physical resources belong to that subhost. Physical injectivity
   6 identifies each rim identifier; spoke restrictions preserve its given type.
   7 This also transfers decompositions between two subhosts of one ambient host. -/
