Lemma 9.1 Successive subsets and external roots
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 8.6

Lean declarations

  • Oberwolfach.vortex_graph_step
  • Oberwolfach.vortex_set_sequence
  • Oberwolfach.nested_normalized_count_estimate

At each step, a set of size \(\lfloor \epsilon |B|\rfloor \) can be chosen inside \(B\) so that every prescribed vertex in a set of at most \(2|B|/\epsilon \) vertices has normalized neighbourhood error less than \(|B|^{-1/3}\). The tested vertices need not belong to \(B\). Successive choices give the two-level root domains of the manuscript.

Proof ▼

Restrict each ambient neighbourhood to the subtype corresponding to \(B\). The fixed-size concentration bound and the union bound give a common successful outcome once \(|B|\) exceeds a threshold depending only on \(\epsilon \). Construct the sequence by induction on its length. Nesting implies that each vertex tested at a later step was tested at every earlier step needed for its telescoping estimate.

Lemma 9.2 Existence of paired vortices
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 9.1

Lean declarations

  • Oberwolfach.vortex_exists
  • Oberwolfach.vertexVortex_exists

For \(\beta ,\rho _*{\gt}0\) and \(0{\lt}\epsilon {\lt}1/4\), there is \(m_0\) such that every \(D\)-regular graph \(P\) on \(n\ge m_*\ge m_0\) vertices with \(D\ge n/2\), and every index set with \(\rho =2|U|/D\in [\rho _*,1]\), admit nested sets \((V_i,U_i)\) with independent recursions \(n_{i+1}=\lfloor \epsilon n_i\rfloor \) and \(u_{i+1}=\lfloor \epsilon u_i\rfloor \). Their terminal vertex size lies in \([\epsilon m_*/2,m_*)\). For \(1\le i\le \ell \) and \(v\in V_{\max (0,i-2)}\),

\[ \left|2u_i/\rho -(D/n)n_i\right|\le \beta n_i,\qquad \left|d_P(v;V_i)-2u_i/\rho \right|\le \beta n_i. \]

The vertex and index types are separate, and no regularity is asserted for the induced graphs.

Manuscript label: def:completion:vortex.

Manuscript label: lem:writing:nested-pairs.

Proof ▼

The accumulated normalized error is at most \(m_*^{-1/3}/(1-\epsilon ^{1/3})\). For \(a=|U|/n\), the two floor recursions give \(|u_i-a n_i|{\lt}1/(1-\epsilon )\). Thus the error in the real degree centre is less than \(3/\rho _*\). The order threshold makes each of these errors at most \(\beta n_i/2\). Nested index sets of the required sizes are constructed explicitly from initial segments of a finite enumeration.

Lemma 9.3 A permutation preserving all vortex levels
✓
#

Uses

  • Lemma 9.2

Lean declarations

  • Oberwolfach.fibre_cycles
  • Oberwolfach.nested_shell_permutation
  • Oberwolfach.permutation_short_interval_injective

For nested finite sets starting with the whole vertex set, suppose each nonempty difference \(V_i\setminus V_{i+1}\) and the terminal set have at least \(h\) vertices. There is a permutation preserving membership in every \(V_i\) such that \(\pi ^k(x)\ne x\) whenever \(0{\lt}k{\lt}h\). Every orbit stays in one of these differences or the terminal set, and \(x,\pi (x),\ldots ,\pi ^{h-1}(x)\) are pairwise distinct.

Manuscript label: lem:writing:shell-permutation.

Proof ▼

Assign each vertex to the largest level containing it, and choose a cyclic permutation on each fibre. The combined permutation is explicitly constructed with its inverse. Its return times at a vertex are exactly the multiples of the fibre’s cardinality.

Lemma 9.4 Vortex estimates on difference domains
✓
#

Uses

Append \(V_{\ell +1}=U_{\ell +1}=\varnothing \). For \(j{\lt}k\le \ell +1\), put \(A=V_j\setminus V_k\) and \(B=U_j\setminus U_k\). For \(v\in V_{\max (0,k-2)}\),

\[ \left|2|B|/\rho -(D/n)|A|\right|\le \tfrac 53\beta |A|, \qquad \left|d_P(v;A)-2|B|/\rho \right|\le \tfrac 53\beta |A|. \]

The same formulas include \(j=0\), using regularity of the initial graph. The added empty level has zero degree errors; no size recursion is asserted at this level. The caller substitutions with \(\beta \le \min \{ \alpha _0/40,\delta _P/16\} \) give the stated comparability, centre bounds and strict degree margin on each difference domain.

Manuscript label: eq:completion:vortex-differences:ordinary.

Manuscript label: eq:completion:vortex-differences:marked.

Proof ▼

The finite-set cardinality and neighbourhood identities give exact subtraction. The error is at most \(\beta (n_j+n_k)\). Since \(n_k\le n_j/4\), this is at most \(\tfrac 53\beta (n_j-n_k)\). Nesting verifies the root domains for both estimates being subtracted.
